# Dubai-Real-Estate-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Dubai real estate listings to uncover pricing trends, neighborhood insights, and property characteristics. The dashboard enables investors, agents, and policymakers to explore price per sqft stability, construction trends, and housing availability through dynamic visuals and slicers.


## Business Objectives

* The dashboard answers the following key business questions:
* How has average price per square foot changed over time?
* Are there significant price differences across neighborhoods and listing categories?
* What is the range of property prices available by year and location?
* Which years saw the highest number of house constructions?
* How do bedroom–bathroom combinations affect pricing and availability?
* Which neighborhoods provide better value per square foot?

## Dashboard Structure & Visuals
#### Section 1: KPI Metrics

Key high-level metrics displayed using KPI cards:

Total Listings

Average Property Price

Average Property Size (Sqft)

Highest Priced Property

Average Price per Sqft

#### Section 2: Visual Analytics
1. Average Price per Sqft Over the Years (Line Chart)
X-axis: Year Built
Y-axis: Average Price per Sqft (measure)

Insight:

Price per square foot has remained largely stable over the past 7–8 decades.
Prices fluctuate within a narrow range of 110–120 per sqft.
This trend holds true across all neighborhoods and listing categories, indicating long-term price stability rather than sharp appreciation or depreciation.

2. Neighborhood Map Visualization

Displays property locations across Dubai
Helps users understand geographic distribution of listings

Value:

Enables location-based decision-making
Assists investors in identifying neighborhood-level price patterns

3. Line & Clustered Column Chart (Price Range Analysis)

X-axis: Year Built
Y-axis: Property Price
Shows maximum and minimum prices per year
Fully interactive with slicers:
Neighborhood
Listing Category
Year

Insight:
Clearly highlights the price range of houses available over time
Helps users compare affordability and premium segments across locations
Supports informed investment decisions based on budget and timeframe

4. Donut Chart – Bedroom & Bathroom Combinations

This is one of the most interactive visuals in the dashboard.

Features:
Three independent slicers specific to this visual

Displays:
Bedroom count
Bathroom count
Number of houses available for each combination
Advanced Tooltips Include:
Average Price per Sqft
Average House Size
Property Price
Total Number of Houses

Insight:

Provides a detailed understanding of how room combinations impact price and availability
Allows granular comparison across neighborhoods and listing categories

5. Bar Chart with Dynamic Top-N Slicer

Displays number of houses constructed per year
Includes a dynamic Top-N slicer (Top 3, Top 5, etc.)
Chart title updates dynamically based on user selection

Insight:

Identifies construction booms in specific years
Helps policymakers and developers understand housing supply trends



## Key Takeaways

* Dubai’s price per square foot has shown long-term stability, indicating a mature real estate market.
* Significant price variation exists across neighborhoods, not across time.
* Investors can leverage price range visuals to match budget with location.
* Bedroom–bathroom combinations play a crucial role in pricing and supply availability.
* Construction trends reveal periods of rapid development.
